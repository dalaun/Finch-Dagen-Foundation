# Promise map: what the old trust drafts promised, and the lawful route for each

SYNTHETIC design note. Research for counsel review, not legal advice. Roles, not names; the old drafts' wording stays in private notes. Every row is traced, with quoted text, in packet/research/Q40.md (claim numbers in the last column).

Dalaun's rule (2026-11-26): the old drafts' fears "should have just been checks". Each promise becomes a row: the lawful mechanism, who provides it, or "no route".
