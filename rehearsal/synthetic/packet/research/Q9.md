# Q9. How do we keep him and his family clear of self-dealing if the trust deducts payments to the foundation?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: If the Family Trust deducts payments to the foundation under 642(c)(1), it may become a 4947(a)(2) split-interest trust. How do we keep the founder and his family clear of self-dealing (4941) with that trust, and should the charitable share be segregated (4947(a)(2)(B))?
- Packet: B
- Confidence: likely
- Lawyer decides: Whether the trust is split-interest on its terms, whether to segregate the charitable share into a separate trust, and how each transaction with the founder's family is cleared.
- Claims:
  1. The 4947(a)(2) rule reaches a non-exempt trust with amounts for which a 642(c) deduction was allowed, and not all of whose interests are charitable.
     - Trace: IRC 4947(a)(2) | "which has amounts in trust for which a deduction was allowed under section 170, 545(b)(2), 642(c), 2055, 2106(a)(2), or 2522" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4947 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. Section 4941, self-dealing, is among the sections listed as applying to such a trust.
     - Trace: IRC 4947(a)(2) | "section 4941 (relating to taxes on self-dealing)" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4947 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. The rules apply to the trust as if it were a private foundation.
     - Trace: IRC 4947(a)(2) | "shall apply as if such trust were a private foundation." | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4947 (read 2026-11-25)
  4. Segregation: the paragraph does not apply to amounts with no deduction allowed, if segregated from those with one.
     - Trace: IRC 4947(a)(2)(B) | "if such other amounts are segregated from amounts for which no deduction was allowable" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4947 (read 2026-11-25)
  5. Self-dealing includes transfer to, or use by or for the benefit of, a disqualified person of the foundation's income or assets.
     - Trace: IRC 4941(d)(1)(E) | "transfer to, or use by or for the benefit of, a disqualified person of the income or assets of a private foundation" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/4941 (read 2026-11-25)
