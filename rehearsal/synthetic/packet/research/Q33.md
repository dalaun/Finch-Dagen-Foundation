# Q33. Is 10 percent the right share of the trust's income for the Foundation, when a 2 percent share still has a break-even and 10 percent has none?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice. SYNTHETIC: no real amounts.

- Question: If the Foundation is to be funded from the trust's income, what yearly share (or fixed dollar amount) is sensible, and can the trust instrument let the trustee choose each year's amount and still get the 642(c)(1) deduction for amounts actually paid?
- Packet: B
- Confidence: open
- Lawyer decides: Whether the instrument fixes the Foundation's share or leaves each year's amount to a trustee, and what that does to the 642(c)(1) deduction; the share itself is a design choice the sources do not set.
- Claims:
  1. A trust (not one meeting the subpart B specifications) deducts gross income that, under the terms of its governing instrument, is paid during the year for a section 170(c) purpose, without percentage limit.
     - Trace: IRC 642(c)(1) | "any amount of the gross income, without limitation, which pursuant to the terms of the governing instrument is, during the taxable year, paid for a purpose specified in section 170(c)" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/642 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. A payment made after year end, by the end of the next year, can be treated as paid in the earlier year if the trustee elects.
     - Trace: IRC 642(c)(1) | "the trustee or administrator may elect to treat such contribution as paid during such taxable year" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/642 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. The permanent set-aside deduction in 642(c)(2) is limited to trusts created on or before October 9, 1969 (and certain wills), so a trust created now is limited to amounts paid under (c)(1).
     - Trace: IRC 642(c)(2)(A) | "created on or before October 9, 1969" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/642 (read 2026-11-25)
  4. The regulation restates the paid-amount rule and points to 642(c)(6) and 1.642(c)-4 for a trust treated as a private foundation under 4947(a)(1).
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 1.642(c)-1(a)(1) | "pursuant to the terms of the governing instrument is paid (or treated under paragraph (b) of this section as paid ) during the taxable year for a purpose specified in section 170(c)" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/1.642(c)-1 (read 2026-11-25)
  5. A taxable private foundation gets the section 170 rules, not 642(c).
     - Trace: IRC 642(c)(6) | "the provisions of this subsection shall not apply and the provisions of section 170 shall apply" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/642 (read 2026-11-25)
  6. Whether an instrument that lets a trustee choose each year's amount meets "pursuant to the terms of the governing instrument".
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: IRC 642(c); Treas. Reg. 1.642(c)-1 and 1.642(c)-3 (read 2026-11-25); 1.642(c)-3 deals with the source and character of the amount, not trustee discretion
  7. Any rule that makes 10 percent, 2 percent or any other share right.
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: IRC 642(c), 4947(a)(2); Treas. Reg. 1.642(c)-1 (read 2026-11-25); the share is an engine setting (`foundation_share_of_distribution`), not a legal figure
