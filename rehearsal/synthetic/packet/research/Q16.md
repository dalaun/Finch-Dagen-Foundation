- Packet: B
- Confidence: open

- Claims:
  1. A single-owner LLC is by default disregarded as separate from its owner for federal tax, so the trust's LLC would be taxed as part of the trust.
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 301.7701-3(b)(1)(ii) | "Disregarded as an entity separate from its owner if it has a single owner." | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/301.7701-3 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. An LLC with two or more members is by default a partnership.
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 301.7701-3(b)(1)(i) | "A partnership if it has two or more members" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/301.7701-3 (read 2026-11-25)
