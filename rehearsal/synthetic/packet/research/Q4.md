# Q4. Can the instrument split the trusteeship (administrative trustee and special distribution trustee) and who serves as independent distribution trustee?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: Can the instrument split the trusteeship (administrative trustee and special distribution trustee) under the Illinois Trust Code, and who is a good independent distribution trustee at a fee the structure can carry?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: none
- Confidence: open
- Assumes: Illinois situs for the Family Trust (see Q22)
- Lawyer decides: Whether the instrument places the distribution power with the independent trustee or advisor in its own terms (claims 2 and 3), and who serves at a fee the structure can carry.
- Claims:
  1. For the federal exception, the distribution power must sit with trustees, none of whom is the grantor.
     - Trace: IRC 674(c) | "by a trustee or trustees, none of whom is the grantor, and no more than half of whom are related or subordinate parties" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/674 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. Illinois law lets the instrument split the jobs. It may name a distribution trust advisor, who by default directs the trustee on every discretionary distribution; the trustee told to follow that direction is an excluded fiduciary, and the power is treated as granted to the advisor, not to the trustee.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(c) | "A distribution trust advisor may be designated in the trust instrument of a trust." | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(c) | "Unless the terms of the trust provide otherwise, the distribution trust advisor has authority to direct the trustee with regard to all decisions relating directly or indirectly to discretionary distributions to or for one or more beneficiaries." | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(a)(2) | "in which case the specified powers are deemed granted not to the fiduciary but to the directing party and the fiduciary is deemed excluded from exercising the specified powers" | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  3. Co-trustees can also divide duties by delegation, but a delegation he makes can be taken back unless it was made irrevocable. So for 674(c) the instrument itself, not a revocable delegation from him, should place the distribution power with the independent trustee.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/703(e) | "A trustee may delegate to a co-trustee for any period of time any or all of the trustee's rights, powers, and duties." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/details?ActID=4001&ActName=Illinois+Trust+Code.&ChapAct=760+ILCS+3%2F (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/703(e) | "Unless a delegation was irrevocable, a trustee may revoke a delegation previously made." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/details?ActID=4001&ActName=Illinois+Trust+Code.&ChapAct=760+ILCS+3%2F (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
