# Q28. What would an unrelated individual trustee really charge, and will one serve?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice. SYNTHETIC: the 1,000 a year fee is a placeholder in stack.json, not a quote from anyone.

- Question: Will an unrelated individual agree to act as trustee or independent distribution trustee of the Family Trust, at what yearly fee, and with what duties and liability?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: none
- Confidence: open
- Lawyer decides: Whether a named unrelated individual will serve, on what fee and liability terms; only the person and the lawyer can settle that, not a statute.
- Claims:
  1. Who counts as "unrelated" for the independence the design needs: a related or subordinate party is presumed subservient to the grantor, so an unrelated person must not be one of those listed.
     - Trace: IRC 672(c) | "to be subservient to the grantor in respect of the exercise or nonexercise of the powers conferred on him" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/672 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. The listed related or subordinate parties include the grantor's relatives and employees, so a sibling or an employee of the grantor does not count as unrelated.
     - Trace: IRC 672(c) | "The grantor’s father, mother, issue, brother or sister; an employee of the grantor;" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/672 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. The independent-trustee exception needs the distribution power held by trustees none of whom is the grantor.
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 1.674(c)-1 | "exercisable solely (without the approval or consent of any other person) by a trustee or trustees none of whom is the grantor" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/1.674(c)-1 (read 2026-11-25)
  4. What an unrelated individual would charge, whether one will serve, and the liability they take on.
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: IRC 672; Treas. Reg. 1.674(c)-1 (neither mentions trustee compensation) (read 2026-11-25); 760 ILCS 3/708 sets only the legal standard (claim 5), not a market rate
  5. Illinois: if the instrument sets no fee, the trustee gets reasonable compensation; if it sets one, a court may allow more or less when the duties change or the fee is unreasonably low or high.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/708(a) | "If the trust instrument does not specify the trustee's compensation, a trustee is entitled to compensation that is reasonable under the circumstances." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/details?ActID=4001&ActName=Illinois+Trust+Code.&ChapAct=760+ILCS+3%2F (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/708(b)(2) | "the compensation specified by the trust instrument would be unreasonably low or high." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/details?ActID=4001&ActName=Illinois+Trust+Code.&ChapAct=760+ILCS+3%2F (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
