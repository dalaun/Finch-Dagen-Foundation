# Q34. Can the founder be paid a one-dollar salary plus fringe benefits by the operating and management companies?

- Question: If the founder takes a token salary from the operating and management companies and receives fringe benefits instead, when is that lawful, when does the IRS recharacterize it, and why can the Foundation or the split-interest trust never pay or provide any of it?
