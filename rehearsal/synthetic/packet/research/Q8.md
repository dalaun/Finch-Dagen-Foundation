# Q8. Does a non-trustee distribution trust advisor satisfy the independent-trustee exception?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: Does a distribution trust advisor under 760 ILCS 3/808 who is not a trustee qualify for the 674(c) independent-trustee exception, or should the advisor be a co-trustee?
- Packet: B
- Confidence: open
- Assumes: Illinois situs for the Family Trust (see Q22)
- Lawyer decides: Whether a non-trustee advisor, an Illinois fiduciary but not a trustee (claim 2), is treated as a trustee for 674(c), or whether the advisor should be a co-trustee; and how any protector power is limited (claim 4).
- Claims:
  1. Section 674(c) speaks of powers exercised by trustees.
     - Trace: IRC 674(c) | "by a trustee or trustees, none of whom is the grantor, and no more than half of whom are related or subordinate parties" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/674 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. Illinois makes a distribution trust advisor a fiduciary with a trustee's duties, and defines fiduciary as including, but not limited to, a trustee. So the statute makes the advisor a fiduciary; it does not make the advisor a trustee.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(e) | "A directing party is a fiduciary of the trust subject to the same duties and standards applicable to a trustee." | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(a)(3) | "means any person expressly given one or more fiduciary duties by the trust instrument, including, but not limited to, a trustee." | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  3. Whether a non-trustee advisor counts as a trustee for 674(c).
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: IRC 674(c) (read 2026-11-25); 760 ILCS 3/808 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26), which settles the Illinois role but not the federal word "trustee"
  4. Illinois lets a trust protector change beneficiaries' interests and remove and appoint trustees and advisors. Either power, if held by him or a nonadverse party with no limit, is a grantor-trust trap the route check already fails (R6 and R7, Reg. 1.674(d)-2).
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(d)(2) | "increase, decrease, or modify the interests of any beneficiary or beneficiaries of the trust" | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 3/808(d)(4) | "remove, appoint, or remove and appoint, a trustee, investment trust advisor, distribution trust advisor, another directing party" | https://ilga.gov/Documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000030K808.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
