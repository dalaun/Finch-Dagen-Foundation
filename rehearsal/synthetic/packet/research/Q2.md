# Q2. Will the Family Trust pay out all of its income each year, or keep part of it?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: Should the Family Trust be written to require distributing its income each year, or leave it to the trustee's discretion, and what does each do to who pays the tax?
- Confidence: likely
- Lawyer decides: Whether the instrument makes the payout mandatory or discretionary; the CPA then reads the tax result.
- Claims:
  1. Distributable net income is the trust's taxable income with listed modifications.
     - Trace: IRC 643(a) | "means, with respect to any taxable year, the taxable income of the estate or trust computed with the following modifications" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/643 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. The trust's deduction for amounts paid out follows what is required to be distributed or properly paid, and is capped at distributable net income.
     - Trace: IRC 661(a) | "any other amounts properly paid or credited or required to be distributed for such taxable year; but such deduction shall not exceed the distributable net income of the estate or trust." | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/661 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. A beneficiary is taxed on income required to be distributed currently, whether or not it is paid.
     - Trace: IRC 662(a)(1) | "The amount of income for the taxable year required to be distributed currently to such beneficiary" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/662 (read 2026-11-25)
  4. Income the trust keeps is taxed to the trust under the section 1(e) rates.
     - Trace: IRC 641(a) | "The tax imposed by section 1(e) shall apply to the taxable income of estates or of any kind of property held in trust" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/641 (read 2026-11-25)
