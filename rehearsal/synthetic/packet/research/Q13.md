# Q13. What must the Family Trust have before it can hold title to an asset?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: For a non-grantor Family Trust under route R2 or R3, what must exist before it can take title to an income-producing asset: instrument terms, trustee acceptance, EIN, Illinois situs, bank account, and who signs for the trust?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: the trust would later hold LLC interests in a business or rental, which could read as a business or investment interest.
- Confidence: open
- Lawyer decides: What the Illinois instrument, trustee acceptance and signing authority must contain; the federal EIN step is traced below.
- Claims:
  1. The trust needs its own EIN (the IRS lists trusts among entities that need one).
     - Trace: IRS, Employer ID Numbers | "You also need an EIN to operate any of these entities:" | https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employer-id-numbers (read 2026-11-25)
  2. Anyone required to furnish an EIN must apply for one.
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 301.6109-1(d)(2)(i) | "Any person required to furnish an employer identification number must apply for one" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/301.6109-1 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. Illinois requirements for creating the trust, trustee acceptance and situs.
     - Trace: pending, needs Dalaun | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=4350&ChapterID=62 (tried 2026-11-25)
