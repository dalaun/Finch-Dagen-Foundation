- Claims:
  1. A non-grantor trust must file an income tax return (Form 1041) if it has any taxable income, or gross income of 600 or more.
     - Trace: IRC 6012(a)(4) | "Every trust having for the taxable year any taxable income, or having gross income of $600 or over, regardless of the amount of taxable income" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6012 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. The fiduciary makes the trust's return.
     - Trace: IRC 6012(b)(4) | "Returns of an estate, a trust, or an estate of an individual under chapter 7 or 11 of title 11 of the United States Code shall be made by the fiduciary thereof" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6012 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. A trust return on a calendar year is due on April 15 after year end.
     - Trace: IRC 6072(a) | "returns made on the basis of the calendar year shall be filed on or before the 15th day of April following the close of the calendar year" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6072 (read 2026-11-25)
  4. A private foundation exempt under 501(a) files an annual return (Form 990-PF) with added private-foundation information, due on the 15th day of the 5th month after year end.
     - Trace: IRC 6033(a)(1) | "every organization exempt from taxation under section 501(a) shall file an annual return, stating specifically the items of gross income, receipts, and disbursements" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6033 (read 2026-11-25)
     - Trace: IRC 6033(c) | "In the case of an organization which is a private foundation" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6033 (read 2026-11-25)
     - Trace: IRC 6072(e) | "a return shall be filed on or before the 15th day of the 5th month following the close of the taxable year" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6072 (read 2026-11-25)
  5. The funder's gift to the trust calls for a gift tax return (Form 709) for the year of the transfer, unless an exclusion or deduction removes it.
     - Trace: IRC 6019 | "Any individual who in any calendar year makes any transfer by gift other than" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6019 (read 2026-11-25)
