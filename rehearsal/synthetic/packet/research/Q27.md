# Q27. How many adult beneficiaries will the Family Trust have?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice. SYNTHETIC: no real people.

- Question: How many adult beneficiaries should the rehearsal assume share the Family Trust's distributions, each taxed at their own brackets (IRC 652(a), 662(a))?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: none
- Confidence: likely
- Lawyer decides: Which beneficiaries the instrument names and in what shares; the number is a family fact for the founder, not a legal rule.
- Claims:
  1. For a trust that must pay out its income currently, each beneficiary is taxed on the income required to be distributed to that beneficiary, whether or not it is paid.
     - Trace: IRC 652(a) | "shall be included in the gross income of the beneficiaries to whom the income is required to be distributed, whether distributed or not" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/652 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. If that exceeds distributable net income, each beneficiary's share is cut in the same ratio.
     - Trace: IRC 652(a) | "there shall be included in the gross income of each beneficiary an amount which bears the same ratio to distributable net income" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/652 (read 2026-11-25)
  3. Amounts keep their character in the beneficiary's hands, and each beneficiary's inclusion is taxed in the beneficiary's own return.
     - Trace: IRC 652(b) | "shall have the same character in the hands of the beneficiary as in the hands of the trust" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/652 (read 2026-11-25)
  4. For a trust that also accumulates or pays out principal, the same inclusion rule applies to amounts required to be distributed currently and to other amounts paid, with a ratio cut if they exceed distributable net income.
     - Trace: IRC 662(a)(1) | "The amount of income for the taxable year required to be distributed currently to such beneficiary , whether distributed or not." | https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/662 (read 2026-11-25)
  5. The number of beneficiaries (two in the engine, one or three in scratch runs).
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: IRC 652, 662 (read 2026-11-25); the count is a family fact, and the sections allocate by whatever beneficiaries the instrument names
