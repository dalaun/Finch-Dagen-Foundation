# Q23. Does the Family Trust, or an Illinois Foundation, have to register or report to the Illinois Attorney General?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: If the Foundation is organized in Illinois and the Family Trust names it as a beneficiary, must the Family Trust itself register or report to the Illinois Attorney General under the Charitable Trust Act (760 ILCS 55), even if the trust is sited in Wyoming or Delaware?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: none
- Confidence: open
- Lawyer decides: Whether naming the Foundation as a beneficiary makes the Family Trust (or its set-aside) a charitable trust holding assets for a charitable purpose in Illinois, and so registrable.
- Claims:
  1. The Illinois Attorney General says all charitable organizations that solicit donations or hold charitable assets in Illinois must register.
     - Trace: Illinois Attorney General, Charitable Giving | "are required to register with Attorney General Kwame Raoul" | https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/charities/ (read 2026-11-25)
  2. Registration uses the CO-1 registration statement and CO-2 financial information form, and registered charities file an annual report (Form AG 990-IL).
     - Trace: Illinois Attorney General, Charity Registration | "Registration Statement for Charitable Organizations." | https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/charities/building-better-charities/charity-registration/ (read 2026-11-25)
  3. The Attorney General's database covers private foundations as well as charities.
     - Trace: Illinois Attorney General, Charitable Giving | "if a charity or private foundation is registered and compliant with reporting requirements" | https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/charities/ (read 2026-11-25)
  4. What the Charitable Trust Act (760 ILCS 55) requires of trustees holding property for a charitable purpose, and whether a non-charitable trust with a charitable beneficiary is covered.
     - Trace: pending, needs Dalaun | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2124&ChapterID=60 (tried 2026-11-25)
  5. Whether the Family Trust must register when sited outside Illinois.
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: illinoisattorneygeneral.gov charities and charity-registration pages (they speak of organizations that solicit or hold charitable assets in Illinois, not of a non-charitable trust naming a charity) (read 2026-11-25)
