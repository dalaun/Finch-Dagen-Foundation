# Q23. Does the Family Trust, or an Illinois Foundation, have to register or report to the Illinois Attorney General?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: If the Foundation is organized in Illinois and the Family Trust names it as a beneficiary, must the Family Trust itself register or report to the Illinois Attorney General under the Charitable Trust Act (760 ILCS 55), even if the trust is sited in Wyoming or Delaware?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: none
- Confidence: open
- Lawyer decides: Whether the trustee of a Family Trust that names the Foundation is a sec. 3 trustee for the Foundation's share, and when the 6-month clock in sec. 6(a) starts for a discretionary beneficiary; whether sec. 6(b) confidential registration fits (it is written for an unmatured remainder or lead interest, and a current discretionary share may not be one); whether a bank or trust company co-trustee, for the sec. 6(a) exemption, is worth its fee; whether sec. 15(a)(5) reaches the trust's non-charitable share or only the Foundation's; and whether Illinois reaches a trust sited in Wyoming or Delaware with an Illinois-resident trustee.
- Claims:
  1. The Illinois Attorney General says all charitable organizations that solicit donations or hold charitable assets in Illinois must register.
     - Trace: Illinois Attorney General, Charitable Giving | "are required to register with Attorney General Kwame Raoul" | https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/charities/ (read 2026-11-25)
  2. Registration uses the CO-1 registration statement and CO-2 financial information form, and registered charities file an annual report (Form AG 990-IL).
     - Trace: Illinois Attorney General, Charity Registration | "Registration Statement for Charitable Organizations." | https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/charities/building-better-charities/charity-registration/ (read 2026-11-25)
  3. The Attorney General's database covers private foundations as well as charities.
     - Trace: Illinois Attorney General, Charitable Giving | "if a charity or private foundation is registered and compliant with reporting requirements" | https://illinoisattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/charities/ (read 2026-11-25)
  4. The Charitable Trust Act reaches any person holding property for a charitable purpose, once the property is worth more than $4,000. Its test is what the property is held for, not whether the holder is a charity, so a trustee of a non-charitable trust holding a share for the Foundation fits the words.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/3 | "holding property for or solicited for any charitable purpose" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K3.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/2 | "holding property of a value in excess of $4,000" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  5. Registration: a copy of the trust instrument, filed within 6 months after any part is received for the charitable purpose and before any disbursement, with a $15 fee; one trustee's signature is accepted.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/6(a) | "within 6 months after any part of the income or principal is received for application to the charitable purpose, and prior to disbursements" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K6.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/5(b) | "A registration fee of $15 shall be paid with each initial registration." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/5(b) | "One signature will be accepted if there is only one officer or one trustee." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  6. The Act expects split-interest trusts: while the settlor is living, a trust registered only because it holds an unmatured charitable remainder or lead interest may ask for a confidential registration, kept out of the public file.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/6(b) | "If the settlor is still living, any trust which is merely registered because it is a charitable remainder interest or charitable lead interest trust, and the charitable interest is not matured" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K6.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  7. A bank or trust company authorized to act as trustee in Illinois, and an individual serving as co-trustee with it, is exempt from the rest of the Act after registering, and files only a copy of the federal return each year.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/6(a) | "shall be exempt from all other provisions and requirements of this Act, but shall be required to annually file a copy of the federal Internal Revenue report or return for the trust" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K6.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  8. Reports: the first report is due within one year after any part is authorized or required to be applied to a charitable purpose. A trustee with more than $25,000 of revenue or assets in a year files an annual financial report, due June 30 for a calendar year; below that, a simplified summary.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/7(d) | "shall be filed not later than one year after any part of the income or principal is authorized or required to be applied to a charitable purpose" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K7.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/7(d) | "if a calendar year the report shall be due on each June 30 of the following year" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K7.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/7(d) | "shall file only a simplified summary financial statement" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K7.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  9. Fees and penalties: $15 with each annual report over the $25,000 line and $100 more if late; a 60-day extension on request; a cancelled registration costs $200 to reinstate; a court may impose a civil penalty of $500 to $1,000 for failing to register or report.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/7(h) | "shall pay a fee of $15 along with each annual financial report" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K7.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/7(h) | "a late filing fee of an additional $100 is imposed" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K7.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/7(g) | "the due date for filing may be extended by the Attorney General for a period of 60 days" | https://ilga.gov/documents/legislation/ilcs/documents/076000550K7.htm (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/5(b) | "accompanied by a re-registration fee of $200" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/5(c) | "a civil penalty of not less than $500 nor more than $1,000" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  10. The Act applies whatever the instrument says, and the register, instruments and reports are open to public inspection.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/13 | "This Act shall apply regardless of any contrary provisions of any instrument." | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/11 | "the register, copies of instruments and reports filed with the Attorney General shall be open to public inspection" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  11. A charitable trustee must avoid self-dealing and must make no non-program loans, gifts or advances to anyone except as the General Not For Profit Corporation Act of 1986 allows. This bears on any plan for the trust to lend to, or guarantee for, a business (Q18).
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/15(a)(1) | "To avoid "self-dealing" and conflicts of interest" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/15(a)(5) | "To not make non-program loans, gifts, or advances to any person, except as allowed by the General Not For Profit Corporation Act of 1986" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  12. A trustee who with malice and without lawful authority uses more than $1,000 of charitable trust funds for personal benefit within 5 years commits a Class 2 felony, with punitive damages and a civil penalty of up to $50,000 per violation.
     - Trace: 760 ILCS 55/17 | "intentionally disburses or causes charitable trust funds to be used for his personal benefit or personal use in an amount in excess of $1,000 within a 5 year period is guilty of a Class 2 felony" | https://www.ilga.gov/legislation/ilcs/ilcs3.asp?ActID=2127&ChapterID=61 (copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26)
  13. Whether the Family Trust must register when sited outside Illinois.
     - Trace: no authority found | searched: 760 ILCS 55 secs 1 to 19 as copied by Dalaun 2026-11-26; no section turns on where a trust is sited. Sec. 3 turns on holding property for a charitable purpose and sec. 13 applies the Act whatever the instrument says, but neither says whether Illinois reaches a trust sited and administered elsewhere (read 2026-11-26)
