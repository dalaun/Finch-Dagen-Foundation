# Q5. Is his power to remove the distribution trustee limited to appointing an independent one?

Research for counsel review, not legal advice.

- Question: Is the founder's power to remove the distribution trustee limited to appointing another independent trustee (Reg. 1.674(d)-2(a))?
- Packet: A
- Coverage flag: none
- Confidence: likely
- Lawyer decides: Whether the drafted removal power is limited enough that its exercise could not change the trust in a disqualifying way.
- Claims:
  1. A grantor's power to remove, substitute or add trustees can keep a trust from qualifying under 674(c) or (d).
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 1.674(d)-2(a) | "A power in the grantor to remove, substitute, or add trustees" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/1.674(d)-2 (read 2026-11-25)
     - Trace: Treas. Reg. 1.674(d)-2(a) | "may prevent a trust from qualifying under section 674 (c) or (d)" | https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/26/1.674(d)-2 (read 2026-11-25)
  2. A power to remove an independent trustee on condition that the grantor substitute another independent trustee does not prevent qualification.
