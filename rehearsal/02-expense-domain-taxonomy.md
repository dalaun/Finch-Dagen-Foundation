# 02. Expense domain taxonomy

[Back to the rehearsal overview](README.md)

This is the list of boxes every transaction gets sorted into before the twin can test it. It is a template. The founder's own counts by domain are kept privately, outside this public repository.

Each domain has a **default layer**: where the line lives if nothing proves otherwise. The default for anything personal is Arm A, because IRC 262(a) disallows personal, living and family expenses unless a section expressly allows them. A line moves only when an instrument in the [register](01-instrument-register.md) carries it and its evidence exists.

## Layers

| Code | Layer | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| L0 | Personal | The founder paying as an individual. Arm A for everything. |
| L1 | Operating companies | the management company, the operating company, the transport company. Real trades or businesses. |
| L2 | Rental property | The building, reported on the owner's Schedule E. Already a business-like layer without any trust. |
| L3 | Family Trust | Holds family assets; pays beneficiaries under the trust instrument. |
| L4 | Foundation | Finch-Dagen Foundation. Programs only. |
| L5 | Estate and legacy | Transfers over time: gifts, exemptions, dynasty, charitable split-interest trusts. |

## Domains

| Code | Domain | Default layer | Candidate routes (see register) | Evidence that moves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Software, AI, cloud, security tools | L0 | L1 under 162; L4 if a program tool | Business or program use log; invoice to the entity; paid from the entity's account |
| D2 | Groceries and household supplies | L0 | L4 only for program supplies | Program record showing the supplies were used by participants |
| D3 | Local transport and parking | L0 | L1 business trips under 162 and 274(d) | Trip log: date, place, business purpose, who met. Commuting has no route |
| D4 | Trading and investing tools, education | L0 | L3 investment expense of the trust if the trust trades its own assets | The trust's own brokerage account and investment policy |
| D5 | News, books, audio and research media | L0 | L1 if research for client work; L4 if program curriculum | Link from the item to a deliverable or program |
| D6 | General online retail | L0 | Sort by line item, then re-route | Line items |
| D7 | Meals | L0 | L1 business meals (generally 50%) | Who, why, where, amount (274(d)) |
| D8 | Building materials and repairs | L2 | L2 already; L1 if the building is moved into an entity | Invoices tied to the unit; repair vs improvement call |
| D9 | Clothing and footwear | L0 | None for ordinary clothing | Only costume or protective gear not suitable for everyday wear |
| D10 | Housing (own residence) | L0 | L1 home office under 280A(c)(1), narrowly; L3 trust-owned residence with fair rent | Exclusive, regular business use; or a lease at market rent |
| D11 | Phone and internet | L0 | L1 business share | Business-use percentage with a basis |
| D12 | Vehicles and fuel | L0 | L1 business share (listed property, 274(d)) | Mileage log |
| D13 | Insurance | L0 | L1 business policies; L3 trust-owned life insurance | Policy owner and beneficiary match the layer |
| D14 | Legal, tax and trust administration | L0 | L1 business advice; L3 trust administration; L4 foundation compliance | Engagement letter naming the client entity |
| D15 | Education and training | L0 | L1 working condition fringe or 127 plan (to $5,250 in 2026) | Written plan; job relation |
| D16 | Family support (tuition, care, living) | L0 | L3 distributions under the instrument's standard; L5 direct tuition and medical payments under 2503(e) | Distribution record against the trust's standard; payment made directly to the institution |
| D17 | Giving | L0 | L4 grants; L5 donor-advised fund, charitable lead or remainder trust | Qualifying distribution record; acknowledgment letters |
| D18 | Program delivery (workshops, curriculum, research) | L4 | L4 | Program plan, participant records, outcome evidence |
| D19 | Compensation to the founder | — | L1 wages; L4 reasonable compensation for necessary personal services, 4941(d)(2)(E) | Role description, hours, market benchmark, board approval without the founder voting |
| D20 | Withheld (sensitive) | L0 | Sorted privately by the founder | — |

## Rules for sorting

1. One line, one domain. Mixed purchases get split by line item, not guessed.
2. The classifier proposes; a person confirms anything below high confidence. Unconfirmed lines stay in L0.
3. Moving a line to another layer is a route, and a route needs its evidence. Relabeling a line never moves it.
4. Housing, food, clothing and personal transport default to "no route" in Arm B unless the line is real program delivery or a documented business share.
