# 05. Questions for the attorney and the CPA

[Back to the rehearsal overview](README.md)

Every amber gate and every stopped optimization step in the twin ends up here. This public version is a generic checklist; the founder's own version, with his entities named, is kept privately. These are written to be read out in a consult. The first group decides whether the stack can be rehearsed on solid facts at all.

Bring: the trust instruments, operating agreements, EIN letters, and any returns filed for the entities.

## Start here: what exists, and how the IRS sees it

1. **the business trust.** It owns the operating company, which runs a contracting business. The regulations say a trust created to carry on a profit-making business is generally not a trust for tax purposes (Reg. 301.7701-4(b)). What is the business trust for tax purposes today, and what returns should it have filed?
2. **Domestic or foreign.** Was any trust in the stack ever formed or treated as a foreign trust? If so, what follows under the foreign-trust grantor rule (IRC 679) and its reporting forms, and what is the clean way to hold everything domestically?
3. **The Foundation.** Has it been organized under state law and has Form 1023 been filed? (Notice is generally due within 15 months of organizing, Reg. 1.508-1(a)(2)(i).) If not, it is not yet a 501(c)(3), and none of the foundation routes exist.
4. **the management company.** What is its tax classification? If it is a single-member LLC with no election, it is disregarded (Reg. 301.7701-3(b)(1)), so it cannot pay me wages or rent from me as a separate taxpayer. Would an S election make sense once revenue supports a market salary?
5. Can you read the the business trust instrument for any language that could get the trust disregarded as a sham, and tell me what to remove?

## The Family Trust

6. Should the Family Trust be a grantor trust or a non-grantor trust? Walk me through: who pays its income tax, whether assets leave my estate, the compressed trust tax brackets, and whether I can be trustee (IRC 672(c), 674(c)).
7. If I live in Illinois, does putting the trust in Wyoming or Delaware change its Illinois tax status (35 ILCS 5/1501)? What would actually have to move: trustee, administration, assets?
8. What distribution standard should the trust use (for example health, education, maintenance and support), and what records does the trustee keep to show each distribution followed it?
9. What asset could go into the trust first, and what gift tax return (Form 709) or valuation would that need?

## The Foundation in the chain

10. The Foundation is the beneficiary of the Family Trust. Does that make the Family Trust a split-interest trust under 4947(a)(2)? My reading is that it applies only if a charitable deduction was allowed. Is any deduction being claimed?
11. Through that chain, does the Foundation end up treated as owning part of the operating company, so that it and its insiders together pass the 20% excess business holdings limit (4943(c)(2)(A))?
12. What personal services could I perform for the Foundation that count as "reasonable and necessary to carrying out the exempt purpose" (4941(d)(2)(E))? How should pay be benchmarked, and who approves it so I am not approving my own pay?
13. Is a private foundation the right vehicle yet, or would a donor-advised fund be cheaper until the stack holds enough assets to fund a 5% payout?

## The operating companies

14. Can the management company adopt an accountable plan now, and will you review the draft (Reg. 1.62-2)?
15. the management company's management fee from the operating company: what documentation shows it is at arm's length (IRC 482)?
16. Could the management company ever function as a family office that is a real trade or business, as in Lender Management (T.C. Memo. 2017-246)? What facts would that take?

## Legacy

17. Once there are assets: dynasty trust with the GST exemption, a grantor trust sale, a charitable lead or remainder trust. Which fits my numbers, and what does each cost to run?
18. Which of these routes, if I use them, should be disclosed on a return (Form 8275) so a challenge would not carry the 40% undisclosed-penalty rate (6662(i))?

## About the legal plan

19. Ask the plan first: are trust documents, entity work and tax questions covered, or only some of them? Plans like this often cover wills and basic estate documents but limit business and tax matters. Check the plan's own terms before relying on it.
