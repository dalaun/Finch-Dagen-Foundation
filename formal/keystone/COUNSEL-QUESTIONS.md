# Counsel questions from the keystone model

Raised by the model in this folder. Roles only. Modeling, not legal advice.

1. **Is the family trust a split-interest trust under 4947(a)(2)?** The branch
   settings say no charitable deduction is claimed for the foundation's share. If
   one is, 4941, 4943 and 4945 apply to the trust as if it were a private
   foundation (except amounts payable under its terms to income beneficiaries),
   and the rule needs to cover the trust's value too.
2. **Substantial contributor timing.** The checks (rule IR10) test the 2 percent
   line at the close of each year a person gave, and once it is crossed treat the
   person as a disqualified person for every spend, even one before the gift that
   crossed it. The statute measures "before the close of the taxable year". Is
   that stricter than the law, and from which day may a purchase from a new big
   donor go ahead? Should a spouse's gifts be added (507(d)(2)(A)), and does the
   founder count as the family trust's creator?
3. **Gifted stock in the founder's company.** If the founder gives the foundation
   stock in his management company, the permitted holding is nil
   (4943(c)(2)(A)). Who can buy it inside the 4943(c)(6) period without a
   self-dealing sale, and does 4941(d)(2)(F) (a redemption on the same terms to
   all holders) fit a closely held company?
4. **Loans and rent-free space from insiders.** 4941(d)(2)(B) and (C) allow an
   insider's interest-free loan and rent-free lease to the foundation. The checks
   hold both for review (AL0). Should they allow them when the use is
   exclusively exempt?
5. **Grantee spending at market from an insider.** An outside grantee, under
   expenditure responsibility, buys supplies at market from a vendor that is a
   substantial contributor to the foundation. The model treats that as the
   grantee's deal, not indirect self-dealing, because the foundation does not
   control the grantee (53.4941(d)-1(b)(1)). Is that right when the grant
   agreement names the purchase?
6. **The founder's pay.** The model allows pay only with a written comparability
   record and only for work done. Who should approve the record so it is not the
   founder deciding his own pay (53.4941(d)-3(c), and check ST3)?
7. **The dated disposal plan.** The checks now require a sale date before the
   4943(c)(6) period ends (PG3c) and block the holding once that date passes
   (PG3d). Is a plan date a year or more ahead of the period's end a sensible
   margin, and when should the foundation ask for the (c)(7) extension instead?
